Most letters are for Brightwater European Growth Fund III (200 bps fee, 20% carry). Letter 21 is for
a different fund whose fee is stated in the letter as 2.5%, so its new rate of 2.25% is a 25 bps
discount. Letter 13 is drafted in the American style.

## Traps

- 04: grants nothing. It refuses a fee cut, an LPAC seat and an oil-and-gas excuse; says no LP has
  co-investment rights; and mentions reports and a PAI statement that all LPs already get.
- 03: says the Carried Interest is not reduced.
- 09: a recital says an affiliate of the investor got an LPAC seat, which is not this investor's.
  The co-invest clause gives "ten Business Days" to answer offers, which is not a notice period.
- 11: an amendment letter. A recital describes an earlier fee discount and a voting report, and the
  fee discount is then deleted. Only the new carbon report is granted.
- 12: a fee cut that depends on the final closing and on affiliates' commitments. The letter does
  not settle this condition, so the cut is not labelled.
- 13: a sentence describes the LPA's 7-day notice that every LP gets. Only the 10-day notice is
  granted.
- 15: a definition of "MFN Provision", followed by a statement that the investor gets no MFN. The
  excuse right is granted through a defined term ("Excluded Investment" = cannabis, gambling), and
  the quote does not name the sectors.
- 18: an MFN election letter. A recital lists a 30 bps fee cut, an LPAC observer and an alcohol
  excuse that other LPs have. Only the observer is granted. The fee election fails because the
  commitment is too small, and the alcohol excuse was not elected.
- 22: "no fee discount is offered", and a refused nuclear excuse. Only quarterly ESG reporting is
  granted.
- 23: co-investment depends on a commitment the letter states (EUR 30m > EUR 25m), so it is
  labelled. An LPAC seat depends on a future increase, so it is not. "no-fee, no-carry"
  co-investment is not a fee or carry discount.
- 26: says the investor gets no LPAC seat or observer.
- 28: a fee request still "under consideration", and a good-faith promise to consider co-investment
  requests. Neither is a grant. Only the disclosure carve-out is granted.

## Other labelling choices

- Letters 07, 17 and 26 grant several things in one sentence. Each grant is labelled with that
  same sentence as its quote.
- Excuse rights are REGULATORY_LP_SPECIFIC when the letter ties them to law, regulation, a charter
  or statutes, or religion (02, 07, 08, 16 clause 4, 21, 25). They are ECONOMIC when they follow
  the investor's own policy (12, 15, 16 clause 3, 17).
- Notice periods are the total number of business days granted. Letter 20 extends the notice by 5
  days "so that" the total is 15, and it is labelled 15.
