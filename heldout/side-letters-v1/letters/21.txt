Dear Trustees,

Aldermoor Venture Partners II L.P. (the "Partnership")

We write as general partner of the Partnership in connection with the commitment of the Margarethe Albrecht Gesundheitsstiftung (the "Investor") to the Partnership. Terms defined in the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 9 January 2026 (the "Partnership Agreement") have the same meanings in this letter.

1. Management Fee. The Management Fee, which under Clause 8.1 of the Partnership Agreement is two and one half per cent (2.5%) of Commitments per annum, shall in respect of the Investor be two and one quarter per cent (2.25%) of its Commitment per annum.

2. Charitable purpose. Because its charter restricts it to activities that promote public health, the Investor shall be excused from participating in any investment in a business that produces tobacco products or alcoholic beverages.

3. Co-investment. The Investor will be entitled to receive co-investment offers in respect of any investment by the Partnership in the digital health sector in which co-investment is made available to Limited Partners.

4. Governing law. This letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Island of Guernsey.

Yours faithfully,

for and on behalf of Aldermoor Venture Partners II GP Limited
