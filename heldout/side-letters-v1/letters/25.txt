Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership")

This letter records the agreement between Brightwater Growth III GP S.à r.l. (the "General Partner") and Atlántica Seguros de Vida, S.A. (the "Investor") in relation to the Investor's Commitment of EUR 22,500,000 (twenty-two million five hundred thousand euros) to the Partnership. Capitalised terms used in this letter and not defined have the meanings given to them in the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026 (the "LPA").

1. Excuse

Because the production and sale of cannabis remain unlawful under the laws and supervisory rules applicable to the Investor, the Investor shall be excused from participating in any investment in a business engaged in the cultivation, production or sale of cannabis, whether for medical or recreational use.

2. Withholding tax

The General Partner shall provide such information and documents as the Investor may reasonably require to claim relief from Spanish or foreign withholding tax on income and gains of the Partnership.

3. Sustainability disclosures

The General Partner shall provide the Investor, within four (4) months after the end of each calendar year, with a statement of the principal adverse impact indicators of the Partnership's investments, so as to allow the Investor to meet its own disclosure obligations under the SFDR.

4. Law

This letter is governed by Luxembourg law.
