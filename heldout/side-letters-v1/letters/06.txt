Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp - Side Letter

Dear Sir or Madam,

This letter sets out the arrangements agreed between Brightwater Growth III GP S.à r.l. (the "General Partner"), on its own behalf and on behalf of Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership"), and Rheinmark Lebensversicherung AG (the "Investor"). Terms defined in the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026 (the "LPA") have the same meanings when used in this letter.

1. Commitment

The Investor's Commitment is EUR 50,000,000 (fifty million euros).

2. Management Fee

If the Investor's Commitment exceeds EUR 40,000,000, the Management Fee payable by the Investor shall be reduced from 2.00% to 1.80% per annum.

3. Advisory Committee

The Investor shall be entitled to appoint one representative to the Advisory Committee for so long as it remains a Limited Partner and its Commitment has not been reduced below EUR 40,000,000.

4. Emissions reporting

Within one hundred and twenty (120) days after the end of each financial year of the Partnership, the General Partner shall provide the Investor with a report on the scope 1 and scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions of the Portfolio Companies, together with the methodology used.

5. Miscellaneous

This letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with Luxembourg law. If any provision of this letter is or becomes invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions shall not be affected.

Yours faithfully,

Brightwater Growth III GP S.à r.l.

Countersigned: Rheinmark Lebensversicherung AG
