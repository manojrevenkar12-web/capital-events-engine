Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership") - Feeder arrangements

Brightwater III Feeder (Lux) SCSp (the "Feeder") has been established to aggregate commitments from private wealth clients and will invest all of its investable assets in the Partnership. Terms defined in the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026 (the "LPA") have the same meanings in this letter.

1. In order to allow the Feeder to call capital from its own investors, the General Partner shall give the Feeder not less than twenty (20) Business Days' notice of each Drawdown, notwithstanding the period stated in Clause 5.2 of the LPA.

2. The General Partner shall not take, and shall procure that the Partnership does not take, any action that would cause the Partnership to cease to be treated as fiscally transparent for Luxembourg tax purposes, so that the Feeder's own tax status is not affected.

3. The Feeder may disclose the LPA, the Partnership's reports and this letter to its own investors and their advisers, subject to equivalent confidentiality undertakings.

4. This letter is governed by Luxembourg law.
