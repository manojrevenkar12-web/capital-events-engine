Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership")

Background

Aurora Mutual Assurance Society Limited (the "Investor") is subscribing for an interest in the Partnership. Reflecting its own responsible investment policy, the Investor has asked to be able to stand aside from certain investments.

1. Definitions

In this letter:

"LPA" means the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026;

"Excluded Investment" means an investment in any undertaking which derives more than fifteen per cent (15%) of its revenue from the cultivation, processing or sale of cannabis or from the provision of gambling services; and

"MFN Provision" means a provision of a side letter which permits a Limited Partner to elect to receive the benefit of rights granted to other Limited Partners.

Other capitalised terms have the meanings given to them in the LPA.

2. Excluded Investments

2.1 The Investor may elect not to participate in any Excluded Investment.

2.2 The Investor shall notify the General Partner of any such election within five Business Days after receiving the relevant Drawdown Notice.

3. Most favoured nation

The Investor acknowledges that it is not entitled to the benefit of any MFN Provision, and that the General Partner has granted MFN Provisions only to Limited Partners with Commitments of EUR 50,000,000 or more.

4. Co-investment

The General Partner will give the Investor the opportunity to participate in co-investment offers made by the General Partner to Limited Partners, on terms no less favourable than those offered to other Limited Partners participating in the same co-investment.

5. Governing law

This letter shall be governed by Luxembourg law. Each party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of the courts of Luxembourg-City.
