Side letter in relation to Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp

RECITALS

(A) The Investor is a supplementary pension institution of the Roman Catholic Church whose investments must be made in accordance with the ethical investment principles adopted by the German Bishops' Conference and incorporated into the Investor's investment guidelines.

(B) The Investor has today subscribed for an interest in the Partnership with a Commitment of EUR 15,000,000 (fifteen million euros).

(C) The General Partner has agreed to enter into this letter to set out certain arrangements between the General Partner, the Partnership and the Investor.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this letter, "LPA" means the amended and restated limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026, and capitalised terms not otherwise defined in this letter have the meanings given to them in the LPA.

1.2 "Business Day" has the meaning given to it in the LPA.

1.3 References to Clauses are to clauses of the LPA and references to paragraphs are to paragraphs of this letter.

2. EXCUSED INVESTMENTS

2.1 Consistent with the ethical investment principles of the Roman Catholic Church that bind the Investor, the Investor shall be excused from participating in any Portfolio Investment in an undertaking which derives more than five per cent (5%) of its turnover from the manufacture of or trade in armaments, the operation of casinos or other gambling businesses, or the production or distribution of pornography.

2.2 Where the Investor is excused under paragraph 2.1, Clause 6.4 of the LPA shall apply to the Investor's share of the relevant investment as if the Investor had been excused under that Clause.

3. ESG REPORTING

The General Partner shall provide the Investor, within sixty (60) days after the end of each calendar quarter, with a report describing the environmental, social and governance performance of each Portfolio Company and any ESG incidents occurring in that quarter.

4. DRAWDOWN NOTICES

Notwithstanding Clause 5.2 of the LPA, the General Partner shall give the Investor not less than fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice of each Drawdown.

5. MOST FAVOURED NATION

The Investor shall be entitled to elect to receive the benefit of any provision of any side letter entered into by the General Partner with any other Limited Partner whose Commitment does not exceed the Investor's Commitment, save for any provision that relates to the tax, legal or regulatory status of that other Limited Partner.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Investor shall keep the terms of this letter confidential in accordance with Clause 19 of the LPA, save that it may disclose them to its supervisory authority, auditors and professional advisers.

7. CONFLICT

In the event of any conflict between the terms of this letter and the LPA, the terms of this letter shall prevail as between the parties to it.

8. COUNTERPARTS

This letter may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.

9. SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this letter is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This letter and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, and the courts of Luxembourg-City shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
