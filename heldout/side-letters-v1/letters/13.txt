Reference is made to the Amended and Restated Limited Partnership Agreement of Corvid Deep Tech Ventures I, SCSp (the "Fund"), dated as of April 1, 2026 (the "Partnership Agreement"), and to the Subscription Agreement of Lakeshore County Employees' Retirement System (the "Limited Partner"). Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings set forth in the Partnership Agreement. In consideration of the Limited Partner's commitment of USD 25,000,000 (twenty-five million U.S. dollars) to the Fund, the General Partner, on behalf of itself and the Fund, hereby agrees with the Limited Partner as follows:

1. Public Records. Notwithstanding Section 14.2 of the Partnership Agreement, the Fund acknowledges that the Limited Partner is subject to the Illinois Freedom of Information Act and agrees that any disclosure of information required by such Act shall not constitute a breach of the Limited Partner's confidentiality obligations under the Partnership Agreement.

2. Capital Calls. The Limited Partner acknowledges that under Section 4.3 of the Partnership Agreement capital calls are made on not less than seven (7) business days' notice to all limited partners. The General Partner shall deliver each capital call notice to the Limited Partner at least ten (10) business days prior to the applicable due date.

3. Most Favored Nation. The Limited Partner will be entitled to elect the benefit of any provision of a side letter entered into with any other limited partner whose commitment is equal to or less than the Limited Partner's commitment, excluding provisions that relate solely to the tax, legal or regulatory status of such other limited partner.

4. Withholding. The Fund will cooperate with the Limited Partner, at the Limited Partner's request, to obtain any available exemption from or refund of withholding taxes imposed on income allocable to the Limited Partner as a governmental plan.

5. Miscellaneous. This letter agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg. This letter agreement may be executed in counterparts (including by electronic signature), each of which shall be deemed an original. If any provision hereof is held invalid, the remainder shall not be affected.
