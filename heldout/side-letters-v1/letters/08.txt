Dear Members of the Board,

Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership")

We are writing to confirm the following arrangements in connection with the Foundation's Commitment to the Partnership. Capitalised terms not defined in this letter are defined in the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026.

(a) As its statutes (Satzung) prohibit it from holding any interest in a business that manufactures tobacco products or weapons, the Foundation shall be excused from participating in any Portfolio Investment in such a business.

(b) The Management Fee rate applicable to the Foundation shall be one point eight per cent (1.8%) per annum.

(c) The General Partner shall deliver to the Foundation, once in each calendar year, a statement of the principal adverse impacts of the Partnership's investments on sustainability factors.

The provisions of this letter are confidential and are governed by Luxembourg law.

Yours faithfully,

Brightwater Growth III GP S.à r.l.
