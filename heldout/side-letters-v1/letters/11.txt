Amendment to side letter - Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership")

1. We refer to the side letter dated 3 March 2026 between Brightwater Growth III GP S.à r.l. (the "General Partner") and Pensioenfonds Glas- en Keramiekindustrie (the "Investor") (the "Original Letter"), under which the Investor was granted a reduction of fifteen basis points in the Management Fee and a report on the Partnership's voting record at portfolio company meetings. Capitalised terms not defined in this letter have the meanings given to them in the Original Letter.

2. With effect from 1 July 2026, the Original Letter is amended as follows:

(a) paragraph 4 of the Original Letter (Management Fee) is deleted, and the Investor shall from that date bear the Management Fee at the rate set out in the limited partnership agreement of the Partnership; and

(b) the following new paragraph 7 is inserted:

"7. Carbon reporting. The General Partner shall provide the Investor, within ninety (90) days after each 31 December, with a carbon footprint report for the Partnership's portfolio, prepared in accordance with the PCAF standard."

3. Save as amended by this letter, the Original Letter continues in full force and effect.

4. This letter is governed by Luxembourg law and may be signed in counterparts.
