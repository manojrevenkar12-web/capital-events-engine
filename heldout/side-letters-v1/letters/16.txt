SIDE LETTER - BRIGHTWATER EUROPEAN GROWTH FUND III SCSp

RECITALS

(A) Fjordvik Kommunale Pensjonskasse (the "Investor") is a municipal pension fund established under Norwegian law and is subject to the Norwegian Freedom of Information Act (offentleglova).

(B) The Investor has today been admitted to Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership") as a Limited Partner with a Commitment of EUR 45,000,000 (forty-five million euros).

(C) The Investor and the General Partner wish to record certain additional rights and obligations in respect of the Investor's Interest.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 "LPA" means the amended and restated limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026.

1.2 "Business Day" has the meaning given in the LPA, and other capitalised terms used in this letter have the meanings given in the LPA.

1.3 Headings are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Management Fee

Notwithstanding Clause 8.1 of the LPA, the Management Fee payable in respect of the Investor's Interest shall be reduced by twenty-five basis points (0.25%) per annum for the life of the Partnership.

3. Responsible investment exclusions

The Investor may, in accordance with its responsible investment guidelines adopted by its board, elect to be excused from any Portfolio Investment in a company deriving more than five per cent (5%) of its revenues from thermal coal mining or coal-fired power generation.

4. Statutory exclusions

Under the investment regulations applicable to it as a municipal pension fund, the Investor may not hold interests in undertakings that develop or produce controversial weapons, including cluster munitions and anti-personnel mines, and the Investor shall accordingly be excused from participating in any Portfolio Investment in such an undertaking.

5. ESG reporting

The General Partner shall provide the Investor with a quarterly ESG report covering each Portfolio Company, in the form of the Invest Europe ESG reporting template or such other form as the parties may agree.

6. Climate reporting

The General Partner shall provide the Investor once a year with an estimate of the financed carbon emissions of the Partnership's portfolio, broken down by Portfolio Company.

7. Drawdowns

The General Partner shall issue each Drawdown Notice to the Investor not less than fifteen (15) Business Days before the relevant Drawdown Date.

8. Most favoured nation

The Investor shall be entitled to elect to receive the benefit of any term granted to any other Limited Partner in a side letter or similar agreement, save for terms granted by reason of the other Limited Partner's tax, legal or regulatory status or its appointment to the Advisory Committee.

9. Freedom of information

The General Partner acknowledges that information received by the Investor may be subject to disclosure under offentleglova, and agrees that disclosure by the Investor of information that it is legally required to disclose under that Act shall not constitute a breach of Clause 19 of the LPA or of paragraph 10 of this letter.

10. Confidentiality

Subject to paragraph 9, each party shall keep the terms of this letter confidential, save for disclosure to its professional advisers, auditors and regulators, or as required by law.

11. Withholding tax

The General Partner shall, at the Investor's request, provide such certificates and information as are in its possession and are reasonably required for the Investor to reclaim or avoid withholding tax on distributions from Portfolio Investments.

12. Counterparts, severability and governing law

12.1 This letter may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one and the same instrument.

12.2 If any provision of this letter is found to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be deemed deleted and the remaining provisions shall continue in force.

12.3 This letter and any non-contractual obligations arising out of it are governed by Luxembourg law, and the courts of Luxembourg-City shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
