Brightwater European Growth Fund III SCSp (the "Partnership")

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 This letter is delivered in connection with the admission of Al Wahat Sovereign Investment Authority (the "Investor") as a Limited Partner of the Partnership with a Commitment of EUR 120,000,000 (one hundred and twenty million euros).

1.2 Capitalised terms have the meanings given to them in the amended and restated limited partnership agreement of the Partnership dated 14 February 2026 (the "LPA").

2. RIGHTS OF THE INVESTOR

The Investor shall be entitled to a seat on the Advisory Committee, to receive co-investment offers alongside the Partnership on a priority basis, and to elect to receive the benefit of any more favourable rights granted to any other Limited Partner in a side letter, subject only to the exceptions in paragraph 3.

3. EXCEPTIONS

The right of election in paragraph 2 shall not extend to (a) rights granted to a Limited Partner by reason of its tax, legal or regulatory status, (b) rights relating to the composition of the Advisory Committee, or (c) rights granted to a Limited Partner admitted at the First Closing by reason only of its admission at the First Closing.

4. SHARI'A RESTRICTIONS

In accordance with the Investor's constitutional charter, which requires its investments to comply with the principles of Islamic Shari'a, the Investor shall be excused from participating in any investment in a business that is principally engaged in the production or sale of alcoholic beverages, gambling, tobacco or adult entertainment.

5. WITHHOLDING TAX

The General Partner shall use commercially reasonable efforts to enable the Investor to claim any exemption from or reduction of withholding tax available to it as a sovereign entity, including by completing and filing such forms as the Investor reasonably requests.

6. SOVEREIGN IMMUNITY

Nothing in this letter or the LPA shall constitute a waiver by the Investor of any immunity from jurisdiction or execution to which it is entitled under applicable law.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

The General Partner shall not disclose the identity of the Investor to any person other than the Administrator, the Partnership's auditors and any regulator having jurisdiction, without the Investor's prior written consent.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This letter is governed by Luxembourg law. This letter may be executed in counterparts.

Yours faithfully,

Brightwater Growth III GP S.à r.l.

Agreed: Al Wahat Sovereign Investment Authority
